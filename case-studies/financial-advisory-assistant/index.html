Illustrative example

An AI agent for a financial advisory firm

Not a client result · Financial advisory

Typical outcome Most routine client questions are answered before they reach the team, and the reply moves from hours to seconds.

The challenge

Picture a boutique financial advisory firm being eaten alive by inbound client questions. Most are not complex — statement explanations, distribution timing, document requests, repeat questions about the firm's process. Each one pulls an advisor away from planning work, and clients wait hours for replies that should take seconds.

The aim is not a chatbot. It is an agent that knows the firm — its philosophy, its language, its compliance posture — and that the firm would be comfortable putting in front of clients.

Our approach

The work starts by reading. A firm like this has years of client emails, a written playbook, FAQ documents, and a compliance manual. Those get sorted with the people who own the client relationship, into questions an agent can answer directly, questions it should only draft, and questions it should hand to a human immediately.

The agent is built against that taxonomy, with a published refusal pattern: clear language for the cases where it will not answer and will route to a person. Compliance reviews every response category before anything faces a client.

What we built

  • A client-facing AI agent grounded in the firm's playbook, FAQ, and process documentation — not a generic chatbot with a logo on it.
  • A three-tier response policy: answer directly, draft for advisor review, or route to a human — with the rules visible and editable by the firm.
  • Source citations on every direct answer, so clients can see which document the agent pulled from.
  • An advisor inbox that surfaces every conversation flagged as sensitive, with the agent's draft already prepared.
  • An admin view that shows weekly question volume, top categories, refusal rate, and any drift in tone — so the firm can keep the agent aligned over time.

Typical outcome

  • Typical outcome: most routine client questions are answered before they reach an advisor.
  • Typical outcome: those replies come back in seconds, not hours.
  • Typical outcome: advisor time comes back to planning work.
  • Typical outcome: sensitive questions route to a person, and the firm can see that they did.

Timeline

  • Week 1: Document review, taxonomy of question types, compliance sign-off on response categories.
  • Week 2: Agent build against the firm's grounded knowledge base, with citations and refusal pattern.
  • Week 3: Internal pilot — agent answered for advisors only, every response reviewed.
  • Week 4: Launch to clients, admin dashboard live.
