Typical outcomes, not testimonials

IguanAI Team · March 31, 2026

These are not clients speaking. The notes below are illustrative examples of the change this kind of work is aimed at, written in plain sentences so the shape of it is easy to see. They are not quotes, and they are not results we are reporting.

Law firm intake. Typical outcome: leads qualify, route, and follow up on their own, and the two days a week that used to go to intake go back to the people who were doing it by hand. The system is a form, a set of scoring rules, a routing logic, and a follow-up sequence. The change is that new business no longer waits on the partners.

Healthcare dashboard. Typical outcome: four spreadsheets become one screen someone can read in the morning and know where the business stands. The hard part is agreeing which numbers are the actual numbers. Once that is settled, the build is fast.

Financial advisory assistant. Typical outcome: most routine client questions are answered before they reach the team, and the reply moves from hours to seconds. The assistant is deliberately narrow. It answers the questions the firm answers every day, and it defers to a human for anything else.

E-commerce onboarding. Typical outcome: the workflow is mapped in one session and running shortly after, and nobody has to keep touching it. Software that needs constant attention has failed at its job.

There is no pattern here other than the pattern we try to make standard: pick a real workflow, build something boring, hand it over, stay out of the way.

— The IguanAI Team
