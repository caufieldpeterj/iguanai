FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to what people ask us most often. If what you need is not here, a fifteen-minute call usually gets to it faster than another email thread.

Engagement

How does IguanAI price engagements?

We work on fixed prices with a defined outcome, not hourly billing. A discovery call is free. An audit is $1,500 and fully credited toward the build. A pilot build is $5,000 fixed: half to start, half when it is running. After the first build, an ongoing partnership starts at $3,000 a month, with a three-month minimum.

We do not charge for strategy decks that cannot be executed.

What is a typical budget range?

A fifteen-minute discovery call is free. An audit is $1,500, credited toward your build. A pilot build is $5,000 fixed and puts one agent live in three to four weeks. Ongoing partnership starts at $3,000 a month, with a three-month minimum.

If budget is a concern, tell us on the first call and we will be direct about whether we can help.

How long do engagements last?

A pilot build goes live in three to four weeks. An ongoing partnership runs three months at minimum, and we ship one improvement a month after the first build. You are not waiting half a year to see something running.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

We are based in Delray Beach, Florida. We travel to clients when the work calls for it — walking a warehouse floor, sitting with a call center team, or being in the room for an executive working session. Most day-to-day collaboration happens remotely.

Process

How does a project start?

With a free fifteen-minute call. If it sounds like a fit, we do an audit: we map the work an agent should take and hand you a prioritized build plan, not a deck. The audit fee is credited toward the build. From there a pilot puts one agent live in three to four weeks.

If we cannot help, we say so and point you somewhere better.

Who on our team do you work with?

An executive sponsor who owns the outcome, and the operators who actually do the work. We build alongside your team rather than over their heads. By the time an engagement ends, your people understand the system well enough to run it and extend it.

What do the deliverables look like?

Working software, with documentation your team can read. Depending on the engagement that is an agent for intake and follow-up, an agent that works across the tools you already use, or a scheduled monitor that reports on its own. A person approves anything sent or spent.

We do not deliver slideware as the final artifact.

AI & Technical

What platform do you build on?

We build primarily on Grok Bot. The platform is chosen for the fit of the client: the workflow, the data, and the tools the work already lives in. We integrate with the systems you already run — commonly Shopify, NetSuite, Salesforce, Snowflake, and AWS. If Grok Bot is the wrong fit, we say so on the first call.

We are independent. We pick the platform that fits.

What is an AI agent vs a chatbot?

A chatbot waits for someone to type. An agent is given a job — qualify an intake, follow up, watch a mailbox, pull a number, report — and it does that job.

It works across email, calendar, documents, and the systems the work already lives in. If it cannot finish without a person, it stops and asks.

Is my data safe, and who approves what the agent does?

The agent only reaches the records you grant it. We document that path so your team can see exactly what it can touch.

Nothing is sent, and nothing is spent, until a person on your side approves it. We do not claim a certification we do not hold. If a control you need is one we cannot honestly provide, we decline the work.

What happens as AI platforms change?

We design the workflow so it survives a change of model and, where it can, a change of platform. The job, the approval step, and the record of what the agent did belong to you. The model is replaceable.

We do not pretend any platform is permanent.

Fit

Which industries do you serve?

We have shipped work in retail and e-commerce, logistics, financial services, healthcare operations, and professional services. The common thread is not the industry but the shape of the problem: a real operational workflow, clear economics, and an executive willing to change how the work gets done.

When is IguanAI not the right fit?

If you want a forty-page strategy deck, a vendor bake-off, or a staff augmentation contract, we are not the right firm. We are also not the right fit if the goal is a demo for a board meeting with no path to production.

We take on a small number of clients and we only take work we believe will actually ship.
